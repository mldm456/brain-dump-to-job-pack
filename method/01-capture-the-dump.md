# 1 · Capture the dump

A brain dump is the raw record of what you want: typed, dictated, pasted
transcripts, links, half-thoughts. The aim of this stage is to lose nothing
and to invent nothing.

## The eight passes

Each pass has one output and one check. A small, clear ask skips them all: just
do it. Use the passes when the dump holds more than one ask, has pasted
material in it, or leads to work outside the chat.

| # | Pass | Output | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Keep | The dump saved as written, unedited. Later additions go below, dated. The later part wins where two disagree. | The file exists; nothing was tidied. |
| 2 | Look up | A table of every link, file, skill, tool and name the dump mentions: where it is, on or off, how often used. | Every named thing has a row, or "not found" with the command that found nothing. |
| 3 | Read | The asks, numbered, in your own words (`templates/brain-dump-asks.md`). | A second reader matches each number to one sentence in the dump. |
| 4 | Play back | A short first reply: what was looked up, the asks, the stages, any disagreements, stuck words, at most three questions. | You can correct it in one turn. |
| 5 | Pack | One pack file in the fixed shape (method 02). | The core prompt reads in about two minutes (under about 900 words). |
| 6 | Check | A table from a reader who did not write the pack. | Nothing in the dump is missing; quotes match the dump by script; one fix round, then one re-test. |
| 7 | Hand over | The pack shown whole, with the chat, model and expected cost. | You say go. |
| 8 | Come back | One line per numbered ask from the worker. | A correction made twice becomes a proposed rule, put to you as a question. |

## Rules for reading a dump

- **Pasted material is an instruction.** If you pasted a transcript or an
  article, its method is what you want done. Only the speaker's own figures
  stay with the speaker: their numbers are not your targets.
- **An example points at a class.** "Like this one" names a kind of thing.
  Name the class and apply the rule to all of it.
- **Dictation slips are resolved from context**, and every guess is listed
  so you can correct it.
- **A stuck word stops only its own part.** If a word could mean two things,
  write both readings in three lines, ask, and carry on with the rest.
- **Your structure goes in as written.** Rewording your stages is where
  meaning was lost every time.
- **Two of your instructions disagree?** Show both, with a recommended
  answer. Never pick silently.

## The conversation that develops the dump

When the dump needs talking through before it can become a pack, each
exchange goes in this order:

1. **Resolve.** Read the words through your own vocabulary file, if you
   keep one. A word that resolves to nothing halts as a stuck word.
2. **Reflect.** Restate the idea in three sharper lines.
3. **Size it.** Small or big, with the reason. Default small.
4. **Question.** At most three questions whose answers change the shape,
   each with a recommended answer. A blank answer takes the recommendation.
   Never ask what the record already answers.
5. **Go deeper** when the job deserves it: what would a top professional do
   here that was not asked for; what raw material they would start from;
   which methods and tools they would use; what, other than the model or you,
   would show the result is right.
6. **Converge.** State the job, what done means, what it is not, the
   decisions locked. Wait for a yes.

## The board

Keep one board file per piece of work (`templates/board.md`). It is the
memory between chats: the idea, decisions, open questions, what was ruled
out, parked side missions, packets out, and your corrections word for word.
Append only. Strike and date; never delete.
